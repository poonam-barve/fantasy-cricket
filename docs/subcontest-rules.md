# Subcontest Rules

This document is the compact source of truth for fantasy subcontests beyond the regular per-match team contest.

## Weekend Battle

- Runs whenever there are matches on Saturday and/or Sunday.
- The last match before Saturday is the qualifier.
- The number of weekend matches decides bracket size: top `2^n` users qualify, where `n` is the number of weekend matches.
- Qualified users are randomly drawn into a fixed knockout bracket.
- Each weekend match is one knockout round.
- Head-to-head results use raw match fantasy points only.
- Score prediction bonuses do not affect bracket results; they remain separate leaderboard bonuses.
- Tie-breaker: higher overall leaderboard rank wins the head-to-head.
- The Weekend Champion gets `+200` leaderboard bonus points.

## Super Team

- One-time playoff squad contest for Matches `71-74`.
- The contest is visible from the dashboard before playoffs, but team selection opens only after the four playoff teams are known from Match `71` and Match `72`.
- Team selection locks at the scheduled start time of Match `71`.
- Each user selects exactly `12` players.
- Pick at least `1` `Wicketkeeper`, `1` `Batter`, and `1` `AllRounder`.
- Select at least `4` Bowlers overall. Bowlers can be from any playoff team.
- Choose one Captain and one Vice-Captain from the selected players.
- Captain points are multiplied by `1.5`.
- Vice-Captain points are multiplied by `1.25` and rounded up to the next `0.5`.
- No backups, substitutes, or Playing XI availability rules apply.
- After Match `72` is completed, the first substitution window opens until Match `73` toss.
- After Match `73` is completed, the second substitution window opens until Match `74` toss.
- First-window penalties compare original team to edited team.
- Second-window penalties compare edited team to final team.
- First-window new player penalties: `80` for Wicketkeeper/Batter/AllRounder and `60` for Bowler.
- Second-window new player penalties: `100` for Wicketkeeper/Batter/AllRounder and `80` for Bowler.
- Captain change penalty is `50%` of the relevant role substitution penalty.
- Vice-Captain change penalty is `25%` of the relevant role substitution penalty.
- Players earn their normal fantasy points in every playoff match they appear in.
- Match `71` and Match `72` use the original team snapshot.
- Match `73` uses the edited team snapshot.
- Match `74` uses the final team snapshot.
- The goal is to pick players whose teams progress, because they can score again in Match `73` or Match `74`.
- After Match `74` is completed, rank `1` gets `+1000`, rank `2` gets `+600`, and rank `3` gets `+300` leaderboard bonus points.
- If multiple users tie at a bonus rank, all tied users get that rank's bonus.
